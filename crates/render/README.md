# render

Putting the timeline's picture together on the graphics card: layers drawn into a frame, and a frame shown. What the layers are at a moment is the engine's business (`engine::frame`); this crate knows nothing of projects or files.

Research and the measurements behind the choices: [docs/research/rendering.md](../../docs/research/rendering.md).

## Status

Built: the compositor, used by the preview worker (`engine::compose`) and the viewer (`ui_kit::video_picture`), picture effects (`effects.rs`: vignette, film grain, sharpen, glow, blur, pixelate, moving the picture inside its frame, mirror, rounded corners, outline, drop shadow, chroma key, letterbox, black and white, sepia, duotone, invert, chromatic aberration, directional and radial blur, glitch, lens distortion, mosaic, kaleidoscope, posterise, halftone and scanlines) and a grade's layers (`Effect::Grade`, in the same file). Titles are drawn on the processor by `engine::title` and placed here like any other picture. Still to come here: the rest of the effects (phase 9, background removal among them) and the rest of the Color page's operations (phase 7).

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `src/geometry.rs` | `Area`: a rectangle in the frame that may be turned (`fitted`: the largest upright one of a picture's shape that fits, centred; `of_part`: where a part of a picture placed, sized and turned by a `Placement` lands; `covers`: whether it hides the whole frame; `scaled`). `Colour`: quick colour as the shader's numbers, made from what the sliders say. `Part`: a part of a picture. No graphics card needed |
| `src/compositor.rs` | `Compositor`: draws layers into a `Frame` (a picture of the timeline's size, in linear light or in display values: `Working`). A layer's pixels are the decoder's planes (two or three, converted to RGB while drawing), RGBA, or a picture the compositor made itself (`Pixels::Made`: a nested timeline's picture, or what is below an adjustment layer, drawn again: its working values unpremultiplied, coloured in linear light and premultiplied again); a frame is drawn over black or, for a picture to be drawn over another, over nothing (`Background`, `draw_over`); it has an area in the frame (which may be turned), a part of the picture to show, a turn to show the stored picture upright, an opacity, a blend `Mode` (Normal, Add, Screen, Multiply, Lighten: a pipeline each, the card's own blending of premultiplied pictures), a second picture to blend in with its share (frame blending: both are turned into light and mixed before anything else, whatever the frame is made in), and quick colour (`Colour`: gains for exposure, temperature and tint, contrast around middle grey, saturation; always worked in linear light). `Shower` draws a finished frame, or the part of it that is seen (`show_part`, for a zoomed-in viewer; `WHOLE` for all of it), into the window or any 8-bit target. Plain wgpu, no interface toolkit |
| `src/effects.rs`, `src/effects.wgsl` | `Effects`: picture effects as passes (and a grade's primary corrections, `Effect::Grade` with `GradeNumbers`: gains for exposure and white balance, highlights and shadows by brightness, contrast around a pivot, the four wheels on display-like values, the curves sampled at 33 places (`CURVE_SAMPLES`), then saturation; mixed with the picture as it came by where it applies, `limits`: a colour range on hue, saturation and lightness of display-like values and an ellipse or rectangle mask, both with soft edges), each reading pictures the compositor made and writing one of the same size; `apply` runs a clip's effects one after another, taking pictures to write into and handing back those used (`Pictures`). `Effect` holds an effect's numbers as the shaders take them: `Vignette` (darker towards the edges in an ellipse of the picture's shape), `Grain` (brightness noise in cells, strongest in the middle tones, a pattern per `seed`), `Sharpen` (pushed away from a blur of the neighbours), `Glow` (what is over a threshold, blurred across and down, added back: four passes), `Blur` (blurred across and down, then taken inside its `Spot` only, softened over a pixel or two at the box's edge), `Pixelate` (blocks lined up with its box's corner, each the colour at its middle), `Move` (the picture enlarged about a point that stays put, turned and shifted inside its own frame; see-through past its edges: what zoom, shake and pan are made of), `Mirror`, `Corners` (see-through outside the rounded box; `leaves_holes`), `Outline` (the most the picture covers within its width, looked for round sixteen directions, in a colour under the picture) and `Shadow` (what the picture covers in a colour, moved, blurred across and down, under the picture), `Key` (a screen's colour, as shown, made see-through: each pixel's colour against its brightness, measured along the screen's less twice its stray to the side; the part of what stays that leans to the screen taken off) `Letterbox` (black bars round a shape in the picture as it came), `Mono`, `Sepia`, `Duotone` and `Invert` (colour looks on light, mixed in by an amount, brightness kept) `Split` (red and blue sampled from either side: chromatic aberration), `Smear` and `Radial` (33 samples along a direction or towards a point) and `Glitch` (bands of some frames torn sideways with their colours apart, a pattern per `seed`), `Lens` (sampled nearer or further from the middle, the corners kept; pinched, see-through past the edges, `leaves_holes`), `Tile` and `Kaleidoscope` (the middle's angle folded into mirrored wedges), `Posterise`, `Halftone` (a dot per cell of a grid turned 45 degrees, its area the cell colour's brightness) and `Scanlines`. An effect that draws past the picture's edges first copies it into a larger picture with room on every side (`Room`); `apply` returns that picture and how much it grew (`Applied`), and mirror and rounded corners keep to where the picture as it came is. Sizes are reference pixels (of 1080 lines of the picture as it came, before any room was made), so a smaller preview looks like the export. Worked on light: a frame in display values is turned into light and back (its glow's blur is rough) |
| `src/caught.rs` | `caught`: runs graphics work with its errors on this thread caught (error scopes) and returned, instead of the library's default of ending the thread; `describe` puts one in a line |
| `src/reader.rs`, `src/planes.wgsl` | `Reader`: a finished frame read back from the card as NV12 for an encoder (BT.709, limited range). The conversion is two small passes on the card; the read waits without holding the device from the window, at most 20 seconds, and a graphics error comes back as `ReadError::Graphics`. `read_back`: the waiting, for any buffers (the scopes' copy uses it too); when it gives up or fails it lets go of the buffers, so they can be copied into again. `black`: a black picture in the same layout |
| `src/layer.wgsl`, `src/show.wgsl` | The compositor's shaders: drawing a layer, and showing a frame (`effects.wgsl` and `planes.wgsl` are with their files above) |
| `src/compositor/tests.rs` | Known colours through the compositor on whatever graphics device the machine has (a software one will do): filling, bars, mixing in linear light and in display values, parts, turned pictures, see-through RGBA, turned areas, two frames blended as light, a picture the compositor made drawn again as it was and coloured |

## Rules

- Layers come premultiplied by their opacity and are laid "over" what is below.
- In linear light, video values become light by the reference display's curve (a power of 2.4) on the way in and go back the same way when shown, so a picture nothing is done to looks exactly as it does drawn directly.
- Nothing here names a file or decodes: pixels arrive as textures on the device the compositor was made for.
