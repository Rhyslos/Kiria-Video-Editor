# engine

Ties the parts together: what is on the timeline at a moment, and the workers that turn it into pictures (and later sound and exported files). The interface only sends requests and reads results back, so slow media can never freeze a window.

## Status

The picture at the playhead (every visible layer, put together on the graphics card), the timeline's sound, playback with the sound as the clock, and a first export to MP4. The rest of export is Phase 6.

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `src/silence.rs` | `find`: the quiet stretches of a sound track from its waveform peaks (gaps count as silent), with a threshold, a minimum length and a little kept at each edge |
| `src/frames.rs` | `FrameCache`: frames kept after they were decoded, found by file and moment (a frame is on screen from its own time until the next one's), within a budget in bytes; room is made by other files' frames first, then by the frames furthest from the playhead (`look_at`). `stretch_start` says where the unbroken kept stretch around a moment begins and `would_keep` whether a frame would stay, for the worker's fill behind the playhead. `budget_on_card`: how much to keep on a graphics card of a given size. The preview worker keeps the frames decoded on the window's device here and looks here before decoding |
| `src/thumbnail.rs` | The project's picture for the launcher: one frame of the timeline (at the playhead, or the first clip's), fitted into 480×270 on black, written as `<file>.thumbnail.png`; `save_frame` writes the frame at a time at its file's full size |
| `src/source.rs` | What the Source half of a split viewer shows: `source` picks the selected clip with a picture (a selected sound clip stands for the picture linked to it) and the moment in its file at the playhead (`within`: its first or last frame when the playhead is outside it), following its speed; `file_alone` makes a project of that file alone, whole, at its own size and rate and looking as it is, so an ordinary preview worker asked for it shows the file raw, with its frame cache and everything else |
| `src/timeline.rs` | `video_at`: the top visible video clip at a time and where in its file that is |
| `src/frame.rs` | `describe`: everything that is seen at a time, as plain numbers: every video track that isn't hidden contributes its clip, bottom first, each fitted into the timeline's frame (bars, never a stretched or cut-off picture) and then placed, sized, turned, cut and coloured as its clip's look says (`placement`, `shown_part`, `colour`; `shown_area` is where what is seen of a picture lands, which the handles on the picture in the viewer outline). Layers that a frame-filling one above them hides are left out, so they are never decoded. `at_size` gives the same picture at a preview's size |
| `src/export.rs` | `Export`: the timeline, or a stretch of it, written as one MP4 on a worker. Each frame's picture comes from a preview worker of its own at the timeline's full size (decoded and put together exactly as in the viewer), is read back as the planes an encoder takes (`render::Reader`), and goes to `media::encode::Writer` with the sound of the same stretch from the playback mixer, to the exact sample. `Quality` sets the bit rate from the size and frame rate. The file is written as `<name>.exporting.mp4` and renamed when complete; cancelled or failed, nothing is left. `progress`, `cancel`, `finished` |
| `src/compose.rs` | `Composer`: puts one moment's decoded pictures together into the timeline's picture on the device the window is drawn with (`render::Compositor`, in linear light). Planes and RGBA in memory are written into textures kept per layer; pictures decoded on the window's device are drawn from where they are (`Composing::wrap`). Finished frames (`Composed`) are drawn into again once nobody shows them. Why the worker does this and not the interface: `docs/research/rendering.md` |
| `src/preview.rs` | `Preview`: a worker thread that decodes the picture at the playhead. Only the newest request counts (scrubbing never queues), also while a picture is being decoded: a decode the newer request can't use is given up between frames, one that is on the way to it carries on, and the decode after one that was given up is always finished, so dragging still gets pictures; when nothing is asked for 80 ms it fills in the stretch behind the kept frames on a second decoder (`fill`), stopping the moment a request arrives; decoders stay open for the four files used last; each file is decoded the fastest way that works for it and steps down when a way fails, at opening or at any picture (`ladder`: on the window's graphics device when the editor hands one over as `OnDevice`, then the GPU's decoder with a copy back, then the CPU), with the reason in the log and no error on screen unless the last way fails too; a file the window's device failed for is not tried there again in the session; `DeviceWatch` is told before the device is asked for anything and how it came back (answered, or an error), for the crash and freeze guard in `graphics`; errors come back with the picture. Started with the window's device (`start_composing`), the worker decodes every layer that is seen (`frame::describe`) and hands back the timeline's whole picture as one finished frame (`Picture::composed`); one layer behaves exactly as a picture alone does, several are each decoded to the end so they show the same moment. Without the device the top clip's picture comes back alone (`Picture::frame`). `Convert` says where colour is converted: pictures come as YUV planes for the viewer's shader when it can take them (in memory, or as images on the graphics card when decoded on the window's device), else as RGBA. `close` stops the worker and waits, briefly, until it has ended: for closing the app, so nothing is still decoding when the window's graphics go away |
| `src/meter.rs` | `Meter`: the master output's peak per channel and its K-weighted loudness over 0.4 s and 3 s (LUFS), fed by the audio thread and read by the interface without locks (`Reading`) |
| `src/mix.rs` | `Mixer`: the timeline's sound at any moment: each clip's own sound first (`volume` from thousandths of a decibel, the bottom of the range silent; `pan_gains`, equal power with the centre leaving the sound as it is; fades on a quarter sine, `fade`, sample-exact from the clip's start and end on the timeline whatever its speed; a clip left as recorded costs nothing), then audible tracks (mute, solo, volume) summed, decoders kept open per file, stream and reading rate (a clip at another speed reads its file faster or slower, tape-style), seeking only when a clip doesn't continue |
| `src/playback.rs` | `Playback`: an audio thread owns the sound card stream and the mixer, keeping a quarter-second ring buffer full; the card's callback never waits or allocates. The position is the samples actually played (the master clock); without a sound card a wall clock runs and playback is silent. The clock stands still until the first sound is ready, so the picture never runs ahead of it, and stopping never waits for the audio thread: the card goes quiet at once and the thread ends by itself. Closing the app does wait, briefly, for those threads to end (`wait_until_quiet`) |
| `build.rs` | Linux debug builds: the tests find the fetched FFmpeg libraries |
