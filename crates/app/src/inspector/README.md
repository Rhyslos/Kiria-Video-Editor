# inspector

The parts of the Edit page's inspector. `../inspector.rs` keeps the panel's state (the values in waiting, each a `crate::pending::Pending`, and what the page takes from it: `seek`, `follow`, `steady`), the project as it would be (`shown`, `sounds_being_set`), the hand on the picture in the viewer (`subject`, `changed_outside`, `let_go`), which clip is shown (`shown_clip`, `effects_target`, `shows_a_clip`), the timeline's rows when no clip is, and `ui`, which runs one frame in named steps: finish what was left on another clip, the header, Title, the picture sections, Effects, Transition, Speed, Audio, then the look's undo step and what Reset and the diamonds asked for (`answer`).

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `look.rs` | A look's numbers as the panel shows them (`Values`: pixels, percent and degrees, read back without changing what wasn't touched), the range each number moves in, Fit (`fitted`, also used by the handles on the picture) and Fill (`fill_percent`), the corner and half layouts (`in_corner`, `in_half`) and each picture section's one-line summary. Pure arithmetic, nothing drawn |
| `keyframes.rs` | `KeyTable`: where each group of the look's numbers has keyframes at the playhead (at one, between, none), the nearest before and after and the ease of the one there, for the diamonds; `over_time`, the "Over time: …" start of a section's summary |
| `picture.rs` | Transform (with the Blend row, Fit, Fill, "On the picture" and the layouts), Crop and Quick color, or Opacity and Quick color for an adjustment layer; the fields each section's Reset takes; `Asks`, what was asked for besides changed numbers; the Blend row (`blend_row`): a picture's blend mode, one `SetBlendMode` step per pick |
| `sound.rs` | The Audio section (volume, pan, fade in and out of the clip's sound clips, set together as one `SetSound`), `SoundValues` and the words its numbers are written and read in ("−3.0 dB", "L 20", "0.50 s · 30 frames"), the header's summary, and which sound clips a clip's Audio sets (`sound_clips`) |
| `effects.rs` | The inspector's Effects section (pictures that play a file or a nested timeline): the effects in order, each with a switch, arrows to apply it sooner or later, ✕ and its settings as sliders (percent; Vignette, Film grain, Sharpen, Glow, Blur and Pixelate, the last two over the whole picture or a box kept inside it; Zoom to a point (with Keep it sharp), Shake, Smooth pan, zooms shown as sizes; Motion blur's shutter in degrees; Mirror's two ticks; Rounded corners; Outline and Drop shadow, a direction in degrees and a colour row; Letterbox's shape as a ratio; Chroma key, its screen colour offered as Green screen and Blue screen; Black and white, Sepia, Invert; Duotone's two colour rows; Chromatic aberration's and Directional blur's amount and direction; Radial blur's amount and point; Glitch's amount and how often; Lens distortion's bend from -100 % to 100 %; Mosaic's, Kaleidoscope's and Posterise's counts in whole steps; Halftone's dot size and amount; Scanlines' darkness and spacing; Light leak's amount, drift and colour; Lens flare's and Speed lines' amount and point; Impact frame's threshold and Inverted tick (the title's swatches and any colour, `title::colour_row`), a tick or a colour one step at once); Add effect; Reset takes them all away. A setting being dragged is shown at once and becomes one `SetEffects` step when let go; clicks are one step at once; every setting row has the look's keyframe control (`At`: the clip and the playhead), a change to a keyed setting being a keyframe at the playhead |
| `speed.rs` | The inspector's Speed section. One speed: the buttons (25 % to 400 %, and for a recording with more frames than the timeline the speed that shows each recorded frame once, 50 % for 120 fps on 60) set it for the whole clip (`SetSpeed`). A ramp: the speed at the playhead with the keyframe diamond at the end of its row (a click adds a speed keyframe at the playhead or removes the one there, and removing the last leaves the clip at the speed it gave; a right-click picks linear, hold or smooth; the arrows move the playhead to the previous or next one), and the buttons then set the speed at the playhead. "Ease into slow motion" and "Slow moment" make a ramp from the playhead (smoothly down to the clean slow speed over half a second, staying there, or for a second and back), keeping what comes before the playhead; "Remove ramp" goes back to one speed, the one at the playhead. Frame blending Off / Blend for pictures. Each is one `SetRamp`, `SetSpeed` or `SetFrameBlend` |
| `title.rs` | The inspector's Title section on a title: its words (a multi-line field; a title just added gets the keys, its words chosen to type over: `Inspector::focus_title_words`), layout, letter size, colour (six swatches and any colour) and band. Words typed and a size dragged are shown at once through `Inspector::shown` and become one `SetTitle` when the field is left, the slider let go or another clip selected |
| `transition.rs` | The inspector's Transition in section, on a picture with a clip ending where it starts (Crossfade in on a sound clip, offering the crossfade shapes): the kind (or None, a plain cut), its length (dragged, typed or one of 0.25, 0.5, 1 and 2 s, no longer than either clip) and Reset; each change one `SetTransition`, a drag one when let go |
