# editor

Parts of the window (`../editor.rs`, `Editor`) with files of their own; each is an `impl Editor` block.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `edit_ui.rs` | The Edit page's layout: the toolbar, the viewer (split view, the slip preview) with the inspector beside it, the timeline with its details, the selected audio track's column (`ui_kit::track_column`: the track of the selected sound clip, else the one it was last for, else the first; its fader and M and S are ordinary `TrackMix` changes) and the master column, the media drawer (`lay_media` lays a file over the timeline, its sound finding free audio tracks, as a drop on a track does), and the full-screen viewer with its scrub bar (each picture cut marked once) |
| `timeline_labels.rs` | Timeline labels as worked on any page: the chosen one (`selected_label`; choosing it lets go of the clips), moving it (`MoveLabel`), its colour from its menu, deleting it (its menu or Delete); labelling the region (`label_region`, Shift+M or the region's menu: a label over the whole stretch, then its name asked for) |
| `timeline_events.rs` | What was done on the timeline turned into commands (moving a clip, also to another track; trims, ripple trims, rolls, slips and slides on frames; splits and the blade; adding, removing, renaming and hiding tracks; dropping a file, effect, title, transition or timeline on it): most build one command from the open timeline (`apply_on_open`, the error reported) at a time on its frames (`on_frame`) |
| `actions.rs` | `run_action`: what every command of the shortcut table does in the window, and `toolbar_action`, the command a button of the timeline's toolbar stands for |
| `windows.rs` | The windows over the pages as the window runs them (a timeline label, the shortcuts page (only its window: the export queue, the cheat sheet and the user guide are followed every frame by `follow_background` in `../editor.rs`, whatever window is open), saved versions, prune, package, templates, remove silence, sync by sound, timeline settings, search, the viewer's own window): each handed what it shows, what was asked done |
| `saving.rs` | Saving: autosave and Ctrl+S handed to workers (a snapshot on the interface thread, the write on a worker, one at a time; the top bar says saving…, the status line Saved. or the failure) and the launcher's picture of the project written after a save (`Thumbnail`; closing waits for one being written, `thumbnail_settled`) |
| `keyframe_clipboard.rs` | Copy and Paste for keyframes: Copy takes the chosen keyframe marks (`TimelineView::chosen_keyframes`) as `project::CopiedKeyframes`, kept in the window for the session, and puts a line saying what was copied on the system clipboard (the window reports a paste key only when the clipboard holds something; pasting it in a text field writes that line); Paste sends `PasteKeyframes` for the selected picture clip (`picture_of`: a selected picture, or the one linked to a selected sound clip) at the playhead, one undo step, and chooses the pasted marks (`pasted_at`, from `CopiedKeyframes::landing`); marks that would land off the clip are left out and the status line says how many. Without marks chosen, something copied or a picture selected, the status line says what to do |
| `clip_clipboard.rs` | Copy, Cut and Paste for clips, on the keys keyframes use: Copy copies the chosen keyframe marks when there are some, else the selected clips with their linked clips (`project::CopiedClips`); Paste pastes whichever was copied last, clips at the edit point of the open timeline (any timeline) as one `PasteClips` step, selecting what was pasted and saying how many were left out (`pasted_says`); Cut copies and removes the selected clips, closing the gap when Settings › Editing › "Cut closes the gap" is on. Also `remove_selected` (Delete and Backspace: `RemoveClips`, or `RippleRemoveClips` closing the gaps on every track) and `close_gap` (a selected gap, or "Close the gap" in its menu: `CloseGap` at the exact edges of the clips the gap names, `gap_edges`) |
| `region_cuts.rs` | The region's cuts, from its right-click menu (in the ruler or on the tracks) or search: remove it closing the gap (`RemoveRangesAndRegion`), remove it leaving a gap (`LiftRange`), keep only it (`RemoveRangesAndRegion` of what is before and after); each one undo step, the region going with the cut (one undo brings back both) except on a lift |
| `unlinking.rs` | "Unlink" (`Command::Unlink`: the clip, with the selection when it is in it, and everything linked to it, a picture and each sound stream apart) and "Link the selected clips" (`Command::Link`) from the timeline's right-click menu; Settings › Editing › "Always unlink audio and video" handed to the document when a project opens and when switched (`Document::set_always_unlinked`), saying in the status line when that unlinked something |
| `undo_says.rs` | Undo and redo with what they did in the status line ("Undone: removed 2 clips in Alt cut.", worked out from the snapshots before and after, `step`) and the timeline they changed opened when another one is on screen (view state, no edit) |
| `timelines.rs` | Several timelines on the Edit page: the timeline tabs beside the timeline's tools (`tabs`: each timeline's name, size, rate and length, and why it can't be placed in the open one; `TabsCache` keeps them between frames until a timeline changes or another opens), opening another timeline (not an edit: `Document::open_sequence`; `follow_open_timeline`, every frame, notices one opening by a tab, a command or undo: playing stops, the playhead goes back to where it was in that timeline this session, nothing stays selected and the timeline is fitted), a new or copied timeline (opened, its name ready to type in its tab), nesting the selected clips, placing a timeline in the open one (from its tab's menu, at the end, or dragged onto the timeline), opening a nested clip's timeline (double-click), and adding an adjustment layer (right-click menu, at the playhead from the shortcut table; it is selected so the inspector shows its colour; the clip selected after adding one or a title is the one that was not there before, `added_clip`, as pasting finds its clips) |
| `clip_job.rs` | What `follow.rs` and `steady.rs` share, a job on one effect of a picture clip run on a thread of its own: finding the clip on the open timeline, its file's picture and the effect (`find`, `Found::last_frame`), the status line as it starts and while it goes on (`started`, `going`, naming the key that stops it), settings in millionths (`millionths`, `share`, `places_in_millionths`), and writing what it found back to the effect as one `SetEffects` (`write_back`); "That clip no longer exists" says what the job was doing (`Doing`) |
| `follow.rs` | "Follow it from here" on a Blur or Pixelate box: starts `engine::track::Following` from the playhead to the clip's end, shows how far it is in the status line (Escape stops it: the shortcut table's `ClearSelection`, which then does nothing else that press, its key named from the table), and sets what it found as the box's Left and Top keyframes (`Effect::following`, one `SetEffects`) |
| `steady.rs` | "Analyse the clip" on a Stabilise effect: starts `engine::steady::Analysing` over the whole clip, shows how far it is (Escape stops it, nothing set, as for `follow.rs`), and sets the view keyframes and zoom it found on the effect, one `SetEffects` |
| `color_ui.rs` | The Color page's layout: the shared viewer (showing a grade being dragged), the bar of the clip being graded, the tools under them, and the timeline with the sound folded in |
| `audio_ui.rs` | The Audio page as the window runs it, its parts laid out by `audio_page::heights` with a handle between each two (`ui_kit::splitter::stack`, the heights a setting): the shared viewer top left, the page's tools top right, the band across the middle (the clarity check, followed every frame, its fixes applied as one undo step each, its findings handed to the timeline as coloured stretches; or a live picture of the sound: the band's histories followed every frame from what was measured, the analyser told what to listen to), the bar (Timeline / Mixer / Split and its direction, Show, the timeline's tools, the lines tool) and the timeline (one video reference row, the audio tracks shown, their lines) and the mixer under it; the meters taken once a frame, the overview's counting, the analyser's track; the line and role events of every timeline |
| `settings_ui.rs` | The Settings window, drawn by `ui_kit::settings` as the launcher's is: tabs Look (theme), Editing (Edit at the playhead, what a click in the ruler moves, Cut closes the gap, Always unlink audio and video, Clip pictures), Playback & proxies (Off / On / Auto, what a jump without a proxy shows first), Audio (the loudness aimed for), Library (where marks are kept), Resources (worker threads and memory for frames, each Auto or a number, and background work, with what is in use now), Cache (disk space for proxies, what is in use, clearing this project's or every proxy, and the waveforms, clip pictures and loudness readings), Graphics (`graphics::SETTINGS`), Keyboard (the shortcuts page), Diagnostics (Diagnostics…, the log folder) and Help (the user guide, `ui_kit::user_guide`: Open shows it in the web browser, as do F1 and "help", "guide" or "manual" in search, `Action::UserGuide`). Each row's id is a constant there; `open_settings_at(id)` opens Settings at one (for the search for commands, and for an error's fix made with `ui_kit::settings::open_at_fix`, such as a picture too large for the graphics card opening the proxy row). A test checks that every "Settings › …" in the editor's and ui-kit's interface text names a real tab. `--settings <id>` and `--settings-search <words>` open it for screenshots |
| `media_removal.rs` | The Library's "Remove from project…" (one file's menu, several files' menu and panel): files no timeline uses go at once (`RemoveMedia`); otherwise a warning (`ask`: how many clips, on which timelines) offers Remove anyway (`RemoveMediaAndClips`, their clips leaving gaps), Remove only the unused, or Cancel. One undo step; nothing on disk is touched |
| `library_ui.rs` | The Library page as the window runs it: hands the page the project, the library index's answer (asked under `library_page::ASKER` while the page is open), proxy states, the labels offered everywhere, the loudness aimed for and the readers of clip pictures and waveforms; does what was asked (label, list and note edits, for one file or several; adding a file, its marked part or several files, `library_page::placing`; proxies; import). `follow_library_page`, every frame: the page's player follows its clock and stops when the page is left, and the timeline's playback stops when the player starts |
| `script.rs` | What an automated run scripts (`run_mode::Automation`, nothing in a normal run): `Script`, the steps the window then takes by itself once the project and its imports are in (`step`, once per frame: `--action` and `--then` commands, `--select-first-clip`, `--sync-by-sound`, `--play`, `--library-tab`, `--package-to`, `--close-request`, and the Audio page's demo: `--audio-tracks` makes the project anew as one undoable `Restore`, `--audio-clarity-region`, `--audio-track`); how the window starts (`settings`, `arrange`: page, drawer, full screen, the Export page's demo queue, Settings and the shortcuts page); what a screenshot waits for and how long |
