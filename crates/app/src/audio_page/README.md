# audio_page

The parts of the Audio page (`../audio_page.rs` keeps the page's state, the tool header, the tool shown, the mixer with the master pinned right, and the value being dragged; `../editor/audio_ui.rs` lays the page out and runs it).

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `eq.rs` | The EQ tool: the response curve of the six bands and the high pass ("Low cut", kept here rather than as a tab: it shapes the same curve), worked out with `audio`'s own filters, over the live analyser (`audio::Spectrum`, the track after its EQ); a numbered point per band to drag (sideways for its frequency, three figures; up and down for its gain, tenths, 0 dB within a quarter), the wheel over a peak for its width, a double-click for flat; Low cut dragged sideways. The chosen band's settings beside it. `x_of`, `freq_at`, `y_of`, `db_at` place things on the graph |
| `dynamics.rs` | The Compressor tool: the gain curve (in, out, the threshold dashed), a gain-reduction meter (`draw_meter`, used by Clean-up too), the plain presets (`preset`, `which_preset`, `preset_menu`, also the master's) and every setting; a number moved on a compressor that is off turns it on |
| `cleanup.rs` | The Clean-up tool: "Learn noise from the region" (or the selected clip of this track; `LearnFrom`), the learned profile drawn across the frequencies, how much is taken now, Amount, how far over the noise passes, Release |
| `ducking.rs` | The Ducking tool: the dialogue tracks it listens to (every track whose role is Dialogue), depth, the speech level that counts, how fast it goes down, holds and comes back, and a live meter of how far down it is |
| `leveller.rs` | The Loudness tool: the loudness the track is kept near, how far it may lift and lower it, and its gain now against those limits |
| `overview.rs` | The full loudness overview (the Meters tab): for the whole mix and the track, integrated loudness large against the aim, then momentary, short-term, range and peak (true peak for the mix; a track's meter has sample peaks only); how they are counted and the aim (the same setting as the master column's and Settings') chosen under them, with Reset |
| `counting.rs` | `Counting` (every play, until reset, in the region; a setting) and `Counted`: readings of the meters every 100 ms of the timeline while playing, tallied (`audio::dsp::loudness::Tally`) for the mix and the track; started again on a new play, a new track, Reset or a changed region as the mode says; jumps (a loop) carry on from the new place |
| `words.rs` | One plain sentence per tool on what it is doing now, from its settings and, while playing, what the mixer measures ("Turns down Ingrid's loudest moments by about 4 dB now, so it stays even.") |
| `roles.rs` | Track roles as the page shows them (names, colours from `ui_kit::theme::role`, their order) and `Show`, the filter that narrows the timeline and the mixer to one role |
| `lines.rs` | Volume and pan lines between the project and the timeline widget: a track's in timeline time, a clip's at moments of its file brought onto the timeline; points coming back go on whole frames, one per frame; `command` makes the undo step for a line let go, `with_line_being_set` the project with a line being dragged (heard while dragging), `fill` hands every track's and clip's line and role to the widget |
| `bar.rs` | How the bar over the bottom views fits its width (`plan`, from real widths measured each frame): the view toggle, Show and the lines tool always; the timeline's tools in a row, or folded into one dropdown; then the mix's loudness, the playhead's time and the needle's, as many as fit whole. Nothing is drawn over anything else |
| `start.rs` | What the command line asks of the page (`--audio-tool`, `--audio-view`, `--audio-track`) and `many_tracks` for `--audio-tracks 30`: the open timeline with that many audio tracks copied from the first one's clips, named, with roles and a few effects and lines, for screenshots and the responsiveness test |
