# ui-kit

Shared interface parts. Anything that must look and behave the same on every page lives here, so pages never draw their own version (working rules 1 to 3).

## Status

Phase 0.6 done: themes, fonts, shared sizes, the top bar, the shortcut table and error display. Phase 2 added the title bar for the launcher, the shared dialog look and the shared time wording. The shared timeline widget (playhead, edit needle, region, labels, ruler, transport) arrives in Phase 4.

Labels: click one to go to it, double-click to edit it (`EditLabel`). `highlights` marks stretches over every track (the silences Remove silence found). Video clips draw their pictures (`ClipPictures`) side by side, each the one nearest its place in the file, with the name on a dark pill. A clip whose speed changes over time hands in where in its file it is along it (`SourceMap`), which its pictures and waveform follow, and its speed (`SpeedCurve`), drawn as a thin line over its lower part. Audio clips draw their waveform (one bar per pixel column); audio track headers have the level button (a line across each clip at its volume: drag it for that clip's volume, double-click for 0 dB, `ClipVolume`; which tracks show it is view state, `level_lines`, and `level_being_set` tells other panels about a drag in progress), M (mute), S (solo) and a volume fader (double-click for 0 dB); waveforms grow and shrink with the clip's and the track's volume, live while the fader moves, and are dimmed on a muted track. Any number of tracks of each kind: a clip dragged up or down lands on the track of its kind under the pointer (`Move` with a `track`; it is drawn there while dragged, and stays on its own track until the pointer is clearly past the row's edge), and a dragged file drops on the video track under the pointer. Dragged clearly past the last track of its kind (above the top video track, under the bottom audio track) a clip asks for a new track (`Move` with `new_track`): a lit strip named "New track" shows where, with the clip in it. A clip dragged to where another clip is in its way is drawn in the warning colour, with the "not allowed" pointer, before it is let go. The video area is as tall as the divider says or, when that is more, as its tracks need, while the audio area keeps three tenths of the height (`fit_video`, `AUDIO_KEEPS`): an added track shows without dragging the divider and a removed one gives its room back; a track that was just added is scrolled into view. The column of track names has a right-click menu (`AddTrack`, `RemoveTrack`, `HideTrack`, and Rename…), a video track's header has an eye that hides it (its clips are drawn dimmed), and a double-click on a name types a new one in place (`RenameTrack`). `rough_cut` gives taller video rows with each clip's pictures on top and its sound below. `compact` draws every track as a strip `COMPACT_TRACK` (18) points tall with each clip's colour and name, the track's name, the eye and M / S (no pictures, waveforms, fades, level lines or fader), leaving the heights given to tracks as they are (resizing waits while it is on). `divider` off makes the video and audio tracks one list that scrolls together (video on top, audio under it, a hairline between them, nothing to drag); `split` and each area's scroll wait for it to come back. Each is one `Keyframes` event (`MarksChange`); `keyframes_being_moved` tells the page about a drag in progress, so the viewer can show it. `click_moves` (`ClickMoves`) says what a click or drag in the ruler moves: the playhead, or the edit needle when the page has one (it is then put down where the drag is let go); the playhead's head at the top of the ruler is a handle that drags the playhead either way (not with Shift, which begins a region), drawn larger with grip lines when a click moves the needle. `toolbar` draws the tool row. `tests/timeline_cost.rs` measures a frame |

## Rules

- Colours come only from `Theme` and `status`. No hex values in pages.
- Sizes that more than one page uses come from `layout`.
- New commands go into the `actions!` list (`src/shortcuts/actions.rs`) and their usual keys into `USUAL` (`src/shortcuts.rs`); the test there refuses a key bound twice.
- Shortcuts match on the physical key, so they work on every keyboard layout. While a text field has focus only Ctrl shortcuts fire.
- Shortcuts can be changed, so interface text never types a key name: it asks the table (`keys_for`, `keys_or_name`, `tip`, `written`), which writes it for the keyboard in use.
- Colours come only from `Theme`, `status` and `context`.
- Every window starts with `setup` (fonts, theme), which also turns off the toolkit's own zoom of the whole interface on Ctrl with =, - and 0: those keys are in the shortcut table (they zoom the viewer's picture).
